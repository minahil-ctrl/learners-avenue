## Purpose

Answer the questions that determine whether — and where — a Learners' Avenue offer wins. Produces the evidence base every downstream subagent relies on.

## Scope (initial, expand after backlog)

- **Demand side:** search volume, community pain points (Reddit, FB parent groups, Quora), waitlists / calendar gaps on existing tutor profiles.
- **Supply side:** teardown of major players — Preply, Wyzant, Superprof, Cambly, and Karachi / Gulf local agencies. Pricing, tutor quality, review patterns, response times.
- **ICP interviews:** talk to real target users (parents, adult learners, students).
- **Segment sizing:** how many, what they pay, willingness to switch.
- **Regulatory scan:** licensing, tax implications for cross-border payments, data privacy for minors.
