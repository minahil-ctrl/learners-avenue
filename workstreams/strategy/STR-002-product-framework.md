## Framework Overview

Five support types across three tracks, plus one universal entry point.

All tracks are open to any student. Track 3 (Mentor Support) drives enrollment through quality — students experience the value, then convert to a Monthly Plan.

The Mentor Check-in is the one product exclusive to Monthly Plan students.

---

## Entry Point (Universal)

### Diagnostic Session
- **Who:** Any student, no prior enrollment required
- **What:** A mentor maps the student's academic gaps, stress points, and goals. Produces a clear recommendation: which track, which products, what to do first.
- **Output:** Written summary + recommended next step
- **Strategic role:** Primary conversion tool. A student who books a Diagnostic and gets a clear plan is primed to enroll. This is the handshake.

---

## Track 1 — Ongoing Support

**For:** Students building mastery across a term or academic year.
**Includes:** Structured sessions (quiz-start → deck → assignment/video close), personalised learning plan, progress tracking.
**Subject scope:** Business Studies, Economics, English Language — CAIE and Edexcel IAL.

### Monthly Plan
- Fixed number of sessions per month (to be confirmed in STR-003)
- Auto-renews
- Best per-session rate
- Priority scheduling — slots reserved before single-session availability
- Includes: Mentor Check-in (see Track 3)

### Single Session
- Pay per session, no commitment
- Same structured format as Monthly Plan
- Higher per-session cost than Monthly Plan
- No Mentor Check-in included
- Natural stepping stone: students who book 3+ single sessions should be offered a Monthly Plan conversion

## Track 2 — Targeted Support

**For:** Students with a specific, immediate need. No enrollment required.
**Available to:** Any student, at any time, regardless of Track 1 status.

### Crash Course
- **Duration:** 3 hours (standard) or 6 hours over 2 days (intensive)
- **Format:** Concept review → past papers + mark scheme breakdown → timed practice + live feedback
- **When:** Booked 1–3 weeks before an exam sitting
- **Scope:** One subject, one paper type or full paper
- **Board-specific:** Tutor knows the exact exam code, mark scheme language, common examiner traps
- **Strategic role:** High urgency, high margin, high conversion. A student who does a Crash Course with LA and gets results is the easiest enrollment conversion.

### Clinic
- **Duration:** 60–90 minutes
- **Format:** Tutor-led, live, one specific problem — one concept, one paper technique, one type of question the student keeps getting wrong
- **Examples:** "20-mark Economics essay structure" / "Case study analysis in Business Paper 3" / "Evaluating in A-Level answers"
- **When:** Any time — not restricted to pre-exam period
- **Scope:** Surgical. One thing, done properly.

### Study Kit
- **Format:** Async, self-study resource pack. Permanent access on purchase.
- **Contains:** Structured notes, worked examples, past paper extracts with annotated mark scheme answers, topic quiz
- **Scope:** One topic or one chapter per kit
- **When:** Any time
- **Cannibalization guard:** Kits cover one topic only. A student would need to buy many kits to replicate what enrollment gives them — making enrollment the clearly better value for ongoing students.
- **Strategic role:** Lead generation. Lowest barrier to entry. A student buys a kit, experiences LA's quality, converts to Diagnostic or enrollment.

## Track 3 — Mentor Support

**For:** Any student — does not require Track 1 enrollment (except Mentor Check-in).
**Strategic role:** Open to all, drives enrollment through quality. A student who has a Clarity Session and feels genuinely helped will want to know what ongoing support looks like.
**Who delivers:** Minahil or a designated senior mentor (not subject tutors).

### Clarity Session
- **Duration:** 45–60 minutes
- **For:** Students who are overwhelmed, burnt out, anxious, or feel lost in their studies
- **What happens:** The mentor listens, identifies what's actually driving the stress (often not the subject — it's time management, fear of failure, family pressure, or poor study habits), and helps the student build a realistic plan to move forward
- **Output:** A short written reset plan — what to focus on this week, what to set aside
- **Not:** Therapy. Minahil is not a counsellor. This is academic mentorship with human awareness.

### Study Strategy Session
- **Duration:** 60 minutes
- **For:** Students who are working hard but not seeing results — the effort is there, the method is wrong
- **What happens:** Review of current study habits, revision approach, time allocation. Mentor diagnoses the method problem and rebuilds the approach with the student.
- **Output:** A written study plan — how to study, when, in what order, using which techniques
- **Examples of what gets fixed:** passive re-reading vs. active recall, poor time distribution across subjects, ignoring mark schemes, not doing timed practice

### Mentor Check-in *(Monthly Plan students only)*
- **Duration:** 30 minutes
- **Frequency:** Once per month, included in the Monthly Plan
- **For:** Students on a Monthly Plan — a regular touchpoint that is not a subject session
- **What happens:** How is the student doing emotionally and academically? Are sessions helping? What's coming up? Any adjustments to the learning plan?
- **Why Monthly Plan only:** This is the benefit that makes a Monthly Plan meaningfully different from buying Single Sessions. It signals that LA cares about the student's whole experience, not just their session count.

## Notes

- The word "module" is retired. Products are named by what they do: Crash Course, Clinic, Study Kit, Clarity Session, Study Strategy Session, Mentor Check-in.
- Track 3 is a genuine differentiator. No competitor in the UAE market offers mentorship or stress support as a named product. It reinforces the "mastery for exams and life" positioning.
- All Track 2 and Track 3 products are acquisition funnels. They exist to demonstrate quality and convert students to Track 1.
