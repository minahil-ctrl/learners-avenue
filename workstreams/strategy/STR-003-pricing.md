## Three Pricing Models

---

### Model 1 — Competitive Entry
*Win on price first, raise later*

| Item | Rate |
|---|---|
| Standard session (1 hr) | **$20 / AED 73** |
| Trial session | **$12 / AED 44** |
| 4-session pack | **$72 / AED 264** (10% off) |
| 8-session pack | **$136 / AED 499** (15% off) |

**Tutor payout:** Rs. 3,000/hr ($10.71)
**Minahil's cut per session:** $20 − $10.71 = **$9.29 (46% margin)**
**At 10 students × 4 sessions/month:** Revenue $800 → Tutor payouts $428 → Minahil keeps **$372/month**

**Pros:**
- Sits at Tutopiya's A-Level rate — price-competitive with the most popular curated platform
- Lowest barrier for the first booking. A parent who's never heard of Learners' Avenue will try a $12 trial with less friction than a $30 one.
- Easy to say in a WhatsApp message: "First session is AED 44, standard sessions are AED 73."

**Cons:**
- Entry pricing signals entry quality. You are telling the parent this is the same as Tutopiya. But Tutopiya is impersonal and generic — you are curated and SA-specialist. Underpricing that gap leaves money on the table.
- Price increases are painful. If you start at $20 and want to move to $30 in month 3, existing students will resist.
- Thin margin means you need volume before Minahil's time investment pays off.

**When to use:** Only if first 3 warm outreach attempts hit price objections. Otherwise, don't start here.

---

### Model 2 — Mid-Premium Curated
*Price at EdFlik, justify with cultural fit + methodology*

| Item | Rate |
|---|---|
| Standard session (1 hr) | **$25 / AED 92** |
| Trial session | **$15 / AED 55** |
| 4-session pack | **$90 / AED 330** (10% off) |
| 8-session pack | **$168 / AED 616** (16% off) |
| Monthly retainer (8 sessions) | **$160 / AED 587** (pay monthly, slight discount) |

**Tutor payout:** Rs. 3,500/hr ($12.50)
**Minahil's cut per session:** $25 − $12.50 = **$12.50 (50% margin)**
**At 10 students × 4 sessions/month:** Revenue $1,000 → Tutor payouts $500 → Minahil keeps **$500/month**

**Pros:**
- $25 is the existing working assumption — no recalibration needed
- AED 92 sits between EdFlik (AED 65–80) and Spires (AED 140+) — justified by curation + SA fit
- 50% margin is healthy. Tutors earn Rs. 3,500 vs. Tutoronics Rs. 2,250 — you become the better-paying option for tutors
- Monthly retainer creates predictable revenue and reduces admin churn (no re-invoicing every session)
- Trial at $15 is accessible but not so cheap it attracts the wrong parents

**Cons:**
- $25 is above Tutopiya's rate. If a parent is comparing, you need to justify the gap. (The answer is: SA cultural fit, structured sessions, personal matching — but you need to communicate this.)
- No price data yet from Ustaad — possible they're lower than $25 and Ustaad serves the same market

**When to use:** Default model. Start here unless there's a specific reason not to.

### Model 3 — Premium Specialist
*Price above EdFlik, signal quality through cost*

| Item | Rate |
|---|---|
| Standard session (1 hr) | **$35 / AED 128** |
| Trial session | **$20 / AED 73** |
| 4-session pack | **$126 / AED 462** (10% off) |
| 8-session pack | **$238 / AED 873** (15% off) |
| Monthly retainer (8 sessions) | **$220 / AED 807** |

**Tutor payout:** Rs. 4,500/hr ($16.07)
**Minahil's cut per session:** $35 − $16.07 = **$18.93 (54% margin)**
**At 10 students × 4 sessions/month:** Revenue $1,400 → Tutor payouts $643 → Minahil keeps **$757/month**

**Pros:**
- Highest margin. Requires fewer students to make the agency financially meaningful.
- Price signals quality — parents who pay AED 128/hr are invested, less likely to cancel
- Tutors earn Rs. 4,500/hr — significantly above market. You attract the best tutors, not the available ones.
- Positions LA above EdFlik without entering Spires/Dubai Tutors territory (AED 140+)

**Cons:**
- Requires proof of quality to sustain. At launch with zero public testimonials, $35 is a hard first sale.
- Significantly higher than Tutopiya — the parent making the comparison will notice. The SA cultural fit angle is not worth $10/hr more to every parent.
- If the trial session is $20, parents may feel the jump to $35/session is steep.

**When to use:** After 10 students and 2–3 documented grade improvements. Or if Minahil's warm outreach contacts are affluent (Dubai-based professionals vs. Sharjah-based families).

## Currency Presentation

**Recommendation:** Quote in USD to UAE parents.

Reasons:
1. UAE parents in professional households think in USD for discretionary services — Payoneer invoices in USD, remittances to Pakistan are in USD, international school fees are often in USD
2. Rs. equivalent looks cheap (Rs. 7,000/session sounds different than $25)
3. AED equivalent is fine for local context but USD positions this as an international-grade service

**On the invoice:** USD amount + AED equivalent in brackets. Example: `$25.00 (AED 91.75)`

## Trial Session Strategy

All three models include a discounted trial. The purpose of the trial is:
1. Remove friction from the first booking (the Sep 15 target)
2. Let the parent experience the structured methodology before committing
3. Give Minahil a chance to assess the student before assigning a tutor

**Trial session rules (regardless of model chosen):**
- One trial per family — not a recurring discount
- Discounted, not free. Free trials attract browsers. Paid trials attract buyers.
- Trial must use the same session format as a standard session (quiz-start, deck, assignment-close) — not a casual intro call
- If the parent doesn't book a second session within 5 days, Minahil follows up once

---

## Package Rationale

Packages serve two purposes: cash flow and commitment. A parent who pays for 8 sessions upfront is not going anywhere. They also reduce per-session admin (one invoice vs. eight).

**Package pricing logic:**
- 4-session pack: 10% off standard rate
- 8-session pack: 15% off standard rate
- Monthly retainer (8 sessions): similar to 8-session pack, but billed monthly and auto-renews. More convenient for ongoing students.

**Do not offer more than 3 package options.** Decision paralysis is a real conversion killer.

## Unit Economics Summary

| Model | Session rate | Tutor payout | Margin/session | 10 students × 4 sessions | Monthly net |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Model 1 (Entry) | $20 | Rs. 3,000 ($10.71) | $9.29 (46%) | $800 revenue | ~$372 |
| **Model 2 (Mid-premium)** | **$25** | **Rs. 3,500 ($12.50)** | **$12.50 (50%)** | **$1,000 revenue** | **~$500** |
| Model 3 (Premium) | $35 | Rs. 4,500 ($16.07) | $18.93 (54%) | $1,400 revenue | ~$757 |

*Monthly net = revenue minus tutor payouts. Does not include Minahil's admin time. Assumes 4 sessions/student/month.*

**Break-even:** Session 1. There are no fixed costs at launch (WhatsApp Business is free, Calendly free tier, Google Sheets for tracking). Every session generates positive cash flow.

**Assumption that breaks this:** If tutors demand more than stated payout rates. Flag this after TUT-001 (tutor spec) is complete.
