# workstreams/market-research/

Outputs of SA-1. Every file here is evidence — sourced, cited, dated.

## Expected files

- `competitor-teardowns/<player>.md` — one per competitor (Preply, Wyzant, Superprof, Cambly, local agencies)
- `icp-interviews/<segment>.md` — interview transcripts + synthesis by segment
- `demand-signals.md` — search volume, community pain points, calendar gaps
- `segment-sizing.md` — TAM/SAM/SOM by candidate niche
- `regulatory-scan.md` — licensing, tax, data privacy
- `research-pack.md` — the final synthesis SA-2 uses

## Rules

- Nothing lands here without a source link.
- Interview evidence beats desk research when they disagree.
- Conflicting signals get flagged, not resolved silently.
