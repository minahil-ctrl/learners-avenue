## 1. Tutopiya — What Minahil Liked (Design Reference)

**Tutors page format:** [tutopiya.com/learning-portal/tutors](https://www.tutopiya.com/learning-portal/tutors)

### Filter system
- Filter by: **curriculum** (CAIE, Edexcel, IB) → **subject** → **grade/level**
- No scrolling through irrelevant profiles — filters reduce the pool before you browse
- Each filter narrows to matching tutors only

### Tutor profile cards show:
- Photo
- Qualifications
- Teaching experience (years + board-specific)
- Hourly rate
- Subject specialties (specific, not generic)
- Student reviews / rating
- Curriculum expertise (which exam board they know)

### Why it works
Parents don't need to read walls of text. The card gives the five things they actually need to make a decision. The filter means they never see a Maths tutor when they're looking for Economics.

**Note for SA-3:** Replicate this filter logic for Learners' Avenue when building the tutor listing page. Minimum filters: subject → level (O/A) → board (CAIE vs Edexcel IAL). Cards must show: photo, subject, board, rate, one-line bio.
