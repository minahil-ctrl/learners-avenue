## Post 2 — Economics insight (credibility)

**Format:** Single image or carousel
**Target date:** Sep 11

```
Most Economics students lose marks not because they don't know
the theory. They lose them because they don't write like
economists.

Evaluation requires you to weigh, qualify, and conclude. Not
just explain. The mark scheme wants "however," "in the long
run," "it depends on whether."

If you're writing explanations without evaluating, you're
capped at Band 2.

If you're sitting Economics this October, this is worth
knowing now.
```
