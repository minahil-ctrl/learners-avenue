# workstreams/tutor-pipeline/

Outputs of SA-4. Supply-side infrastructure.

## Expected files

- `sourcing-plan.md` — channels, targets, cadence
- `screening-rubric.md` — subject test, mock session, background check
- `contract-template.md` — contractor vs. employee — needs legal review
- `rate-cards.md` — what tutors earn
- `onboarding.md` — first-week playbook
- `pilot-cohort.md` — the 5–10 tutors ready for launch

## Rules

- No onboarding without screening.
- No contract without legal review.
- Data privacy enforced — tutors handle minors' info.
